### Featured Research

**CW-Node**
A connection weighted node architecture study, part of a broader hypothesis that network nodes should be small dense networks (nano MLPs) that communicate through a shared pool, rather than single scalar activations.
Repo: [cw-node-research](https://github.com/mohamedhossammohamed/cw-node-research)
Results: [mohamedhossammohamed.github.io/cw-node-research](https://mohamedhossammohamed.github.io/cw-node-research/)

**ANATOME**
A benchmark where AI models generate 3D human anatomy specimens (Three.js) from curriculum prompts, judged blind by the public and scored by me against my own medical curriculum.
Operated under [@MohamedHz72007](https://x.com/MohamedHz72007).

**Streaming Anomaly Detection**
A streaming time series anomaly detection system, methodology designed by me and implemented via AI coding agents. Benchmarked on TSB-AD-U (350 series), reaching a 70 percent win rate against a persistence baseline on VUS-ROC.

**CIRLS-GLM Reproduction**
An independent reproduction of Masselot et al.'s CIRLS-GLM paper, with the reproduction confirmed by the paper's lead author.

**Chimera Agent**
Contribution to a team project for a medical AI Grand Challenge on prostate cancer risk and survival modeling. Built a browser based dashboard for cohort statistics, risk scoring, and embedding based clustering.
